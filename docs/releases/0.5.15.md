# HomeWindow 0.5.15

- **Weather from a weather app on Homey.** When you sign in with your Homey account, Homey does not give its own weather to other apps, so the weather card stayed empty. HomeWindow now takes the temperature, condition and humidity from a weather app on your Homey (such as OpenWeatherMap or Open-Meteo, or a weather station): the first device by name that measures the temperature and also rain or wind, so a sensor indoors does not count.
- **A notice when there is no weather.** When the weather is switched on (in the panel or in the taskbar widget) but neither Homey nor a weather app gives any, the Overview says how to get it.
