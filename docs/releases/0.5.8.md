# HomeWindow 0.5.8

- **Hide a consumer on the Energy page.** Right-click a device under the biggest consumers to leave it off both lists and the taskbar widget. It still counts in the home's use. "Show hidden devices" brings them back.
- **Lights in the panel.** The panel's last tab can show every light per room instead of the moods (Settings → Tab next to Flows).
- **Camera pictures large.** Click a picture on the Overview to see it large; Esc or a click closes it.
- **Sliders can be dragged again** in the device details, for example the target temperature, and stop on the device's step.
- **Device types once.** Types with the same title, such as several kinds of "Other", are one filter on the Devices page.
- **Filters and search.** Variables and Notifications get a filter; search boxes get a clear button and wait until you pause typing, so long lists filter faster.
- **Notifications:** select several and delete them at once.
- **Batteries** are shown in columns.
- **Panel:** no star on every item under Favourites, and a second click on an open device closes its details.
- **Tray icon:** double-click opens the window.
