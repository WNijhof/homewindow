# HomeWindow 0.5.6

- **Taskbar widget keeps clear of your icons.** With many programs open, the widget next to the notification area could sit on top of the taskbar icons. It now knows where the icons end and shows fewer of the biggest consumers when room is short, or hides when even that does not fit. It also no longer shows up on the right for a moment before moving to the left.
- **Hide a camera picture.** Some cameras give Homey more than one picture (Eufy: the last event and the last person seen). Right-click a picture on the Overview and choose **Hide**; **Show hidden pictures** next to the heading brings them back. A camera with more than one picture says which one each tile is.
