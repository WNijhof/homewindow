# HomeWindow 0.4.6

- **Choose which solar devices count.** If Homey has more than one solar device for the same panels, such as an inverter and a kWh meter on it, HomeWindow added them up and showed too much solar and too much house use. With two or more solar devices, the Energy page now has **Solar counts from**, where you switch off the ones that are counted twice. The live numbers and the kWh totals both follow it.
- **Larger text in the taskbar strip.** Settings → **Strip text** → **Large**.
