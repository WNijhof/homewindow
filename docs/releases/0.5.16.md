# HomeWindow 0.5.16

- **Flows and moods as a list in the panel.** The list/grid button in the panel by the clock only switched the devices. Flows, favourite flows and moods now follow it too: as a list, one per row over the full width.
- **Fold away the camera pictures, or hide a whole app.** Click the **Camera pictures** heading on the Overview to fold the row in or out; HomeWindow remembers it. Right-clicking a picture now also offers **Hide all pictures of this app**, for apps whose pictures are no camera, such as the maps of a weather app. **Show hidden pictures** brings them back.
