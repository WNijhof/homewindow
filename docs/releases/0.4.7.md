# HomeWindow 0.4.7

- **A tile shows only the details you chose.** A device without its own state, such as a car charger without on/off, showed temperature and humidity as its status, with your chosen details after them. Now the details you pick take their place.
- **Notifications say HomeWindow again.** On some PCs, Windows titled HomeWindow's notifications "NotifyIconGeneratedAumid_…". HomeWindow now registers its own name and icon with Windows.
- **EV charger.** An EV charger gets its own card on the Energy page, with the power going into the car and, if the charger reports it, the car's charge. The totals show what the car charged today and this month. It can also be shown in the taskbar widget while the car charges: Settings → Appearance → **What the taskbar widget shows** → **EV charger**.
- **The taskbar strip is now called the taskbar widget**, also in the settings.
- **Device icons from Homey.** Devices show the icon they have in Homey, including one you picked in the Homey app. An icon you choose in HomeWindow still comes first. Switch it off under Settings → Appearance → **Icons from Homey**.
