# HomeWindow 0.4.7

- **A tile shows only the details you chose.** A device without its own state, such as a car charger without on/off, showed temperature and humidity as its status, with your chosen details after them. Now the details you pick take their place.
- **Notifications say HomeWindow again.** On some PCs, Windows titled HomeWindow's notifications "NotifyIconGeneratedAumid_…". HomeWindow now registers its own name and icon with Windows.
