# HomeWindow 0.5.17

- **Long weather lines wrap.** Rain and wind from a weather app could be cut off on the weather card ("Rain next 6 hours 3,50…"). The line now continues on the next one, and breaks between two values, never inside one.
- **More from a weather app.** A weather device's feels-like temperature, the chance of rain next to the rain amount, and the wind with its direction and gusts now show on the weather card. Apps that number their wind (the yr.no app: `measure_wind_strength1`) are picked up too, and so is the yr.no condition.
- **Humidity and pressure as a choice.** Settings → **Humidity and pressure** leaves them off the weather card.
- **Weather in the taskbar tooltip.** Pointing at the taskbar widget now shows the rain and wind above the graph too.
- **Right means right.** With a taskbar tool such as StartAllBack, the widget went before the program buttons even with **Place on the taskbar: Right**. It now only does that with **Left**; with **Right** it sits next to the notification area.
