# HomeWindow 0.4.8

- **Start and stop charging from the EV charger's tile.** A charger without on/off, which starts and stops charging with a switch of its own, now gets the same button on its tile as in the Homey app.
- **No phantom kWh for an EV charger.** A charger could show up in *Use today per device* with the kWh of its last session, without having charged. HomeWindow now reads the charger's kWh meter that Homey Energy uses, and ignores a kWh counter that drops out and comes back, or jumps by more than a home can use in that time.
- **Power in W or kW.** Choose under Settings → Appearance → **Power**: *Only W*, or *kW from 1000 W* (3182 W then reads 3.2 kW). It applies to the energy figures, the taskbar widget and the power of devices.
