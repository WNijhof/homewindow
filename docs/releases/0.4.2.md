# HomeWindow 0.4.2

An update to the alpha, with thanks to everyone who left feedback on the Homey Community forum.

- **PIN lock.** Keep colleagues and guests on your laptop away from your home: Settings → *Security* → **Set a PIN** (4 to 12 digits). HomeWindow then asks for it at start-up, when Windows locks, after a while without use (15 minutes by default) and with **Lock now** in the settings or the tray menu. A padlock at the top of the panel and the main window pauses the lock without forgetting the PIN. The PIN itself is never stored, only a salted hash.
- **Your Homey favourites.** HomeWindow now takes over the favourite devices and flows from the Homey app the first time it connects, so a new installation no longer starts empty. Settings → General → **Import** adds them again later. Nothing is removed, and nothing is written back to Homey.
- **Extra small tiles**: Settings → Appearance → *Tile size* → **Extra small**, four tiles a row in the panel by the clock.
- **Collapse all** and **Expand all** for the rooms are now also in the panel by the clock, not only in the main window. An empty favourites tab explains what it is for.
- **The taskbar strip after a restart.** When Windows refuses HomeWindow a place in the taskbar, which happened after closing and starting it again, the strip now sits on top of the taskbar instead of disappearing. It hides for full-screen apps and an auto-hidden taskbar.
- **The installer speaks English** on a Windows in a language it has no translation for, such as German. It used to fall back to Dutch.
