# HomeWindow 0.5.0

- **Biggest consumers in the taskbar widget.** Settings → **What the taskbar widget shows** → *Biggest consumers* adds the top three devices by power right now, so a charger or a dryer shows up by itself while it draws power. It follows the W/kW setting.
- **Home battery on the Overview.** A card with the charge in percent, a bar, and whether the battery is charging, discharging or idle, with its power.
- **Control favourites from the keyboard or a MIDI controller.** `Ctrl + 1` to `9` presses the quick button (on/off, lock, …) of the first nine favourites, in the panel and in the window. Under Settings → General, a switch turns on MIDI input: the notes from middle C (60) up press the same favourites. The controller is plugged into this PC and needs no connection to Homey. Nothing happens while HomeWindow is locked with a PIN.
- **Camera pictures on the Overview.** The latest picture Homey keeps for a camera device (apps such as Eufy, Reolink and Ring), refreshed about every 30 seconds while a window is open. It is a first version: if your camera app does not give Homey a picture, there is simply no camera section. Tell us which camera you use if yours does not show.
