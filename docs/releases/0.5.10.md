# HomeWindow 0.5.10

- **Lights keep their own view.** List or grid on the Lights page no longer changes the Devices page, and the other way round. In the list, every row has the same width.
- **Hidden camera pictures come back at once** after "Show hidden pictures", instead of after the next refresh.
- **Open log** with no errors so far now opens a log that says so, rather than an empty folder.
- **Support:** a small Ko-fi button at the bottom of Settings, for anyone who wants to buy me a coffee.
- **Diagnostics:** the report now says where the taskbar widget was placed and which taskbar buttons it found, to help with a widget that covers a taskbar button.
