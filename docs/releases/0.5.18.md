# HomeWindow 0.5.18

- **Tidier weather lines.** Rain from a weather app now has one line of its own, with one decimal ("Rain 0.5 mm (71 %) · Next 6 hours 0.7 mm (55 %)"), and the wind the line below it, in whole numbers with the direction first and the gusts in brackets. Humidity and pressure come last. The feels-like temperature moved up, next to the condition under the temperature.
- **A shorter tooltip on the taskbar widget.** Pointing at the widget no longer repeats the numbers the widget already shows, and never shows a part you switched off for the widget. What remains: the weather in words with rain and wind, whether the home battery charges or discharges and at what power, and the graph.
- **"max" in the graph.** The number at the top right of the graph is the highest use in the period, and now says so.
