# HomeWindow 0.3.0 – first beta

The first public beta of HomeWindow: control your Homey Pro from the Windows taskbar. Feedback is very welcome in the [issues](https://github.com/WNijhof/homewindow/issues).

**Download** `HomeWindow-Setup-0.3.0.exe` below. Windows may say the publisher is unknown (the installer is not code-signed): choose *More info → Run anyway*. Already running 0.1 or 0.2? HomeWindow updates itself.

## New in this version

- **Tiles like the Homey app** – a round quick-action button in the colour of the device, a coloured state (on, power, music, heating, locked) and a click on the tile opens the details. Choose per device which measurement the tile shows, plus an optional second one.
- **Three tile sizes** – Small, Normal and Large, tested in the main window and the panel.
- **Energy on the taskbar** – use, solar and grid next to the clock; returned power shows as a negative number in green, everywhere in the app.
- **Dashboard style** – the look of the Homey energy dashboard, with a soft glow in the colour of where your power comes from right now. It is the new default.
- **Energy page** – a new layout with equal blocks and less empty space.
- **App icons** on the System page, in each app's brand colour.
- Rooms inside other rooms line up with the rest on the Devices page.
- The processor meter is gone from the System page.

# Nederlands

De eerste openbare beta van HomeWindow: bedien je Homey Pro vanuit de Windows-taakbalk. Feedback is welkom bij de [issues](https://github.com/WNijhof/homewindow/issues).

- **Tegels zoals in de Homey-app**: een rond snelknopje in de kleur van het apparaat, een gekleurde status, en een klik op de tegel opent de details. Per apparaat kies je welke meting de tegel toont, en eventueel een tweede.
- **Drie tegelgroottes**: Klein, Normaal en Groot.
- **Energie in de taakbalk**: verbruik, zon en net naast de klok; teruglevering staat overal als negatief getal in het groen.
- **Dashboard-stijl**: de stijl van het Homey-energiedashboard, nu de standaard.
- **Energiepagina** opnieuw ingedeeld, **app-iconen** bij Systeem, kamers in kamers netjes uitgelijnd, en de processor is weg bij Systeem.
