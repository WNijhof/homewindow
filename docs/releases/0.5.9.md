# HomeWindow 0.5.9

- **Lights in the main window.** A new Lights item in the sidebar shows every light per room, with search.
- **Long lists show faster.** Flow and device tiles share one right-click menu instead of building one per tile.
- **Panel:** the Flows and Moods tabs are filled right away, also when the panel is opened for the first time after the data has loaded.
- **Diagnostics:** a panel tab that takes longer than 1.5 seconds to show is noted in the log, with the number of flows, folders and devices.
