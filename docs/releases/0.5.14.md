# HomeWindow 0.5.14

- **Rain and wind on the weather card.** Homey's own weather has no rain or wind. If you have a device that measures them (a weather station, or an app such as Buienradar), the weather card on the Overview now shows them on a line of their own. With more than one, the first by name counts. A value of its own from such an app, such as rain in the next hour, keeps the name the app gives it. The card now also shows when only such a device has weather data.
