# HomeWindow 0.4.1

A small update to the alpha.

- **Energy at the left of the taskbar.** With the taskbar icons in the centre (the Windows 11 default), the energy strip next to the clock got in the way of your apps on a full taskbar. It now sits at the very start of the taskbar, after the Widgets button. Prefer it by the clock? Settings → Appearance → *Place on the taskbar* → **Right**. When your taskbar icons are aligned to the left, or it does not fit, it stays on the right.
- HomeWindow now remembers when it first ran on a PC.
- A `CONTRIBUTING.md` for anyone who wants to help.
