# HomeWindow 0.4.1

A small update to the alpha.

- **Energy on the left of the taskbar.** With the taskbar icons in the centre (the Windows 11 default), the energy strip next to the clock can get in the way of your apps. Settings → Appearance → *Place on the taskbar* → **Left** puts it at the very start of the taskbar instead, after the Widgets button. When it does not fit there, or your taskbar icons are aligned to the left, it stays on the right.
- HomeWindow now remembers when it first ran on a PC.
- A `CONTRIBUTING.md` for anyone who wants to help.
